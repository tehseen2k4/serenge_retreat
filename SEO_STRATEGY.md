## 3. Metadata Standards (Emotional Triggers)

We use emotional triggers and power words like **"★"** (Star) and **"✓"** (Checkmark) to increase Click-Through Rate (CTR).

- **Titles**: [Primary Keyword] - [Compelling Hook] | Brand ★ (55-60 chars)
- **Descriptions**: [Action Verb] + [Benefit]. [Keyword naturally included]. Clear CTA Here ✓ (155-160 chars)
